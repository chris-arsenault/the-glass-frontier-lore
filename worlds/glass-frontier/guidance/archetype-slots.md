# Archetype Slots — The Glass Frontier

Which archetypes in the Kaleidos system are full and which are thin. This is what `craft/writing-guidance.md` §1 asks for when it says not to default to a known entity: check the category before attributing anything. The schema behind the counts is `craft/entity-bank-schema.md`; the live roster is the `world/` DSL.

The automated archetype-gap report was retired with the graph. Use judgement and the roster.

## Thin — reach for a `future` marker instead

- **Criminal syndicate** — Coremark is the only named one. Smuggling, black-market channels and protection rackets should attach to unnamed networks or a `future` marker, not to Coremark by default.
- **Trade corporation** — Vantara is the only named one. Routine freight, contracts and logistics belong to unnamed commercial carriers unless the entry is about Vantara specifically.
- **Mercenary company** — sparse. Hired force is usually a crew, not a company anyone has heard of.

## Full — attribute freely where accurate

- **Governance body** — the Tempered Accord, the Displacement Council and the Shear Compact cover the system. Naming one of them is fine where it is the right answer.

## The Habit

If an archetype has N filled and M expected and N < M, treat the named entities as *one of several* rather than *the one*. The Kaleidos system is much bigger than the entities written so far, and every unnamed carrier or syndicate that gets pinned to Vantara or Coremark makes it smaller.
