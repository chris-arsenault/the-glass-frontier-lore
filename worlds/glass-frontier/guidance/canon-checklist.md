# Canon Checklist — The Glass Frontier

The fixed facts a crosswalk review checks entries against. Procedure is `craft/crosswalk-review.md`; this file is the answer key. The authority for all of it is `world/timeline.rb` and the entity files — if this table and the DSL disagree, the DSL wins and this file is wrong.

## Era Boundaries

| Era | Span (CE) |
|---|---|
| The Ring Age | –2140 |
| The Glassfall & Signal Famine | 2140–2305 |
| The Rekindling | 2305–2340 |
| The Contested Reach | 2340–2378 |
| The Silent Bloom | 2378–2407 |
| The Bitter Reach | 2407–2423 |
| The Accord | 2423–present |

Present day is 2435 CE. Any "roughly N years ago" in prose must land against that.

## Dates That Have Been Got Wrong Before

- **The Silent Bloom begins in 2378**, not 2355. A round of entries carried the earlier date and had to be corrected across seven files.
- The Signal Famine runs the length of the Glassfall era — 165 years. Entries that say "a century" are wrong.
- The elves vanish *during* the Signal Famine, not at the Glassfall itself.
- **The ring broke 295 years ago**, not 130. The elapsed spans below were all wrong in prose written before the timeline was extended, in seventeen places across ten files. Any new span gets checked against this table.

## Systems Vocabulary That Must Stay Consistent

- **Resonance bands** — structural (low), kinetic (mid), signal (high). Always these three, always in this relationship. Check any entry that names a band.
- **Ringglass** — sourcing, grades, processing and trade routes are fixed. New entries that consume ringglass must draw on the existing supply chain rather than inventing a parallel one.
- **Kites** — motive power by momentum nullification. Not thrust, not sails in the aerodynamic sense.
- **Tuner abilities** — stated limits. No new tuner capability without a cost and a ceiling.

## Facts Entries Get Loose About

- The elves are **known history**, not a mystery. The open question is where they went. See `tone.md`.
- The Tempered Accord is a **framework for mediation**, not a government. Entries that describe it governing anything are wrong.
- The Displacement Council's neutrality comes from staying out of the Bitter Reach. That is why it is trusted, and it is why it is stretched thin.
- Coremark is a criminal syndicate now, publicly discredited after the Bloom.
