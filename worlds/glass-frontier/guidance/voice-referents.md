# Voice Referents — The Glass Frontier

The substitutions `craft/voice-review-prompt.md` asks for. The Kaleidos system has no Earth: no Sol, no Venus, no Terra. Every comparison must reach for something inside the world or for a universal physical concept.

## Banned Referents

Real-world vehicles and services — trucks, taxis, cabs, Uber, sailboats, trains. Real-world places and bodies — Sol, Venus, Earth, any continent or country. Real-world institutions and brands of any kind. Real-world historical periods used as shorthand ("medieval," "Victorian," "industrial revolution").

## In-World Substitutions

| Reaching for | Use instead |
|---|---|
| a taxi, a cab, a rideshare | a hab-runner, a lift-crew, a berth on someone's kite |
| a truck, freight haulage | a hauler, a route carrier, a Vantara contract |
| a sailboat as a luxury | a private kite, a pleasure hull |
| "Venus-like," "hellish planet" | describe the pressure, the heat, the sky |
| "medieval," "pre-industrial" | name the era: pre-ring, Rekindling-era, Famine-era |
| the sun | Kaleidos's star, or the light itself |

## What Is Not Leakage

Universal physical concepts — gravity, pressure, crystalline structure, resonance, orbit. General English that is not Earth-specific — city, river, war, trade, harbour, guild. In-world analogies comparing one thing in the Kaleidos system to another; those are the good kind and there should be more of them.

## The Test That Actually Works

Read the comparison aloud as a Shear salvager would say it. If they would have to have visited a world they have never heard of to understand it, rewrite it.
