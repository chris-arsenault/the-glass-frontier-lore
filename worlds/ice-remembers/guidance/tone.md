# Tone

## Entries

Write encyclopedia prose from inside Aurora Berg. State what a place contains,
what a person did, how a practice works, and who disputes an account. Use ice,
water, current, pressure, warmth, route, witness, ledger, echo, pod, name,
scar, tool, and offering when those are the concrete facts.

Do not turn every entry into an omen or a war story. The published record also
contains work, trade, law, festivals, teaching, repair, family, exploration,
religious practice, and arguments about evidence. Keep that range.

The ice records events without judging them. A reader interprets what was
recorded and can be incomplete, biased, or wrong. Name the reader, institution,
pod, witness, or surviving record when an interpretation matters.

## Cultures

Aurora life values public witness, procedure, accumulated names, and a record
that others can inspect. Show the work that sustains those practices and the
ways public memory can confer or deny standing.

Nightshelf life values controlled disclosure, incomplete names, protected
knowledge, warm routes, and skilled work below the ice. Do not reduce it to
criminality, deception, or an aesthetic of vague darkness.

Orca life happens within pods, hunting grounds, trade routes, currents, songs,
scars, and remembered deeds. Orcas have interior lives and political and
religious disagreements. Do not describe them as weather, animals acting on
instinct alone, or a single hostile culture.

The Wake-Singers are an orca religious order. Their rites do not define every
orca, and rare penguin adherents do not make the order a separate people.

## Magic

Keep the three sources distinct:

- Aurora-light works through perception, ice, crystal, and memory. Deep use
  erodes the practitioner's clarity of self.
- Captured fire releases force and heat from fire-cores. Its burns and lasting
  bodily damage are material costs.
- Pressure-depth works through descent, pressure, song, blood, and communion.
  Sustained use leaves the practitioner emotionally distant from those nearby.

Show the tool, material, bodily action, and cost. Do not use a general aura or
unexplained energy as a substitute.

## Chronicles and annotations

Imported accepted chronicles keep their complete published wording and form.
They may be stories, reports, assessments, or other documents. Their public
historian notes can correct, challenge, or comment on the text and remain in
the historian's own voice.

New chronicle prose does not explain its lesson at the end. Stop after the last
action, observation, or quoted judgment that belongs to someone in the world.
